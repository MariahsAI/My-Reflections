How it works

One sentence in. A finished song out.

You don't need to sing, write or know music. You need something that happened to you.

Type or say a moment. A person, a night, a text you never answered.

Pick a sound

Sad indie, hyperpop, bedroom pop, club, lo-fi. Or a look, if you want a film.
